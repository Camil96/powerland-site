Service and maintenance for charging stations

 

Schedule a technical consultation

Powerland monitors your charging infrastructure remotely via the my.powerland platform, resolves faults remotely where possible and sends a technician when an on-site intervention is required. As a certified service partner of Kempower, Alpitronic and Ekoenergetyka,  with experience across all common brands — including Alfen, Smappee and Etrel — Powerland provides service for private individuals, businesses and public charging infrastructure throughout Belgium.

Certified partner of Kempower, Alpitronic and Ekoenergetyka

Powerland is a certified service partner of Kempower, Alpitronic and Ekoenergetyka , the leading manufacturers of DC fast chargers in Europe. This means direct access to factory knowledge, original parts and specialised firmware updates for your fast charging infrastructure.

Our approach

Remote monitoring

Your charging point is continuously monitored via the my.powerland platform.

Diagnostics & updates

Many faults are resolved remotely — faster than an on-site intervention.

On-site intervention

Our technicians visit when remote support is not sufficient.

Priority based on impact

High-impact faults, such as public fast chargers, are given priority.

How does the Powerland service desk work?

Report a fault by phone or email. Powerland assesses every report on urgency and priority. Where possible, the issue is resolved remotely via the my.powerland platform: diagnosis, reset or firmware update. Is an on-site intervention required? Powerland will schedule a technician visit. Need shorter service response times? Our sales team offers a tailored solution.
