Charging services, management and invoicing

Installing a charging point is the first step. After that, you want to know who is charging, how much it costs and how to manage consumption smartly. Via the my.powerland platform, you manage all your charging points from one environment — whether you charge at home, open your station to third parties, manage a vehicle fleet or operate a public charging plaza.

1 Platform, 4 benefits

Insight into every charging session

Real-time overview of consumption, status and users per charging point

Invoicing and billing

Automatic invoicing for visitors, split billing for home chargers

Access control

Determine who charges, when and at what rate via RFID or whitelist

Smart control

Load balancing, priority control and integration with PV, battery or EMS

Who are charging services for?

Private individuals

You charge at home and want insight into your consumption. Or you open your charging point semi-publicly and let visitors pay via QR code or charge card. Via the my.powerland platform you retain full control.

Businesses

You manage charging points for employees, visitors or pool vehicles. Insight per user, vehicle or department. Home charging billed via split billing. Optionally semi-public at your own rate.

Public locations

You operate public charging infrastructure, such as a charging plaza or car park. Your charging points appear on the public Powerland charging map and invoicing to users is handled automatically.

Management via the my.powerland platform

Real-time dashboards

  • Status of all your charging points
  • Active sessions
  • Consumption per location in one overview

Consumption analysis

  • Detailed insight per user, per vehicle or per time period
  • Export reports for fleet, finance or HR

Rate management

  • Set whether charging is free or paid
  • Differentiate per user
  • Work with time windows

Invoicing and cost allocation

Invoicing for users

  • Visitors and third parties pay via QR code or interoperable charge card
  • Rates are transparent and adjustable

Split billing

  • Home charging sessions of employees with a company electric car are automatically recorded and billed to the employer.

Reporting

  • Overviews per department, per vehicle or per location
  • Ready for export to accounting or payroll

Smart charging through integration with energy management

Dynamic load balancing

  • Prevents peak consumption
  • Optimises available capacity

Priority control

  • Operational vehicles first
  • Then pool vehicles and staff

Integration with EMS, PV and battery

  • Charging on solar energy
  • Buffering via battery storage
  • Optimisation during off-peak hours

The my.powerland platform works via OCPP, the open standard protocol for charging infrastructure. This means you are not tied to one brand of charging station and manage all charging points from one environment. Do your employees also charge on the road? With the Powerland charge card, they can charge at more than 850,000 points across Europe. One card, one invoice, full overview.

Yes, if your charging point is public or semi-public. You can activate payment via QR code or another payment solution via the my.powerland platform.

Every charging station must communicate with an online platform to start, stop, invoice and monitor charging sessions. OCPP - Open Charge Point Protocol - is the open standard that regulates that communication. Because OCPP is an open protocol, you are not tied to one manufacturer: you can manage charging stations from different brands via one platform.

Do you only charge your own car at home? Then a charging station can work fine standalone, without a platform. But in some households there are two electric cars in the driveway - one of which is a company car. Do you want the charging sessions of that company car to be charged to the employer? Then you need a management platform, and thus a charging station that supports OCPP. With Powerland you take out a subscription to our platform for this. A rate is set on your charging station, the driver charges at home with the employer's charging pass, and Powerland pays you out for those charging sessions. The employer receives the invoice via their charging pass provider - everything is handled automatically. And because Powerland also supplies charging passes, you can arrange everything with one partner if desired: charging station, platform and charging pass.
