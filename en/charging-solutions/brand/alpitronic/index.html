Alpitronic is a leading brand in fast charging infrastructure for electric vehicles. The company, based in Bolzano (South Tyrol), combines German quality with Italian design in its innovative hyperchargers. These fast chargers use the latest technologies and meet high standards of reliability, safety, and ease of use.

Alpitronic hyperchargers are known for their high charging speed, efficiency, and minimal energy losses, especially when using cooled cables. Thanks to their modular design, they can be easily adapted and later expanded with additional power via power stacks. This makes them not only suitable for current needs but also future-proof. Through continuous innovation, Alpitronic has quickly developed into a key player in the rapidly growing electric mobility market.
