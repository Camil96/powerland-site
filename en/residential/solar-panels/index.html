Generate your own electricity and pay less for energy

Solar panels remain one of the most profitable investments for homeowners. You generate your own electricity, reduce your energy bill and future-proof your home. Powerland provides a reliable installation, fully tailored to your roof, consumption and plans.

Request a quote

Why choose solar panels for your home?

Lower energy bill

You consume less electricity from the grid

Fast payback period

On average 4 to 7 years, depending on consumption and roof

Increased home value

Better EPC rating and more attractive when selling

Less dependent

Generate a large share of your own electricity

Every quote is tailored to your home and consumption.

What do solar panels cost for a home?

Prices depend on:

  • type of panels
  • type of inverter
  • mounting and roof type
  • shading and orientation
  • distance to the meter cupboard
Contact us

How many solar panels does your home need?

This depends on:

  • your annual consumption in kWh
  • the available roof surface area
  • orientation and pitch angle
  • shading on the roof
  • future plans (electric car, heat pump)

How does a solar panel installation work?

Solar Panels

Convert sunlight into electricity (direct current).

Inverter

Converts this current into usable electricity for your home.

Monitoring via app

You monitor production and consumption in real time.

Combining solar panels with a home battery

With a home battery, you increase your self-consumption from 30–40% to 60–80%.

Benefits:

  • less electricity injected into the grid
  • use more of your own energy in the evening
  • lower energy bill
  • ideal in combination with a charging station or heat pump

Which homes are best suited for solar panels?

  • Homes with a pitched or flat roof
  • Households with average to high consumption
  • Those planning an electric car or heat pump
  • New builds and renovations
