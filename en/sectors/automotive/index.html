Energy and charging solutions for automotive: dealers & leasing

 

Request project advice

Receive a tailored proposal

Smart charging, EMS, battery storage and solar panels for showroom, workshop and delivery zone, plus a partner offering for leasing.

More EVs means more charging moments, higher peaks and a greater need for control. Dealers want vehicles ready to go at all times; leasing companies are looking for a reliable partner that can handle installation, expansion and service. Powerland combines charging infrastructure, EMS, battery storage and PV into one working system, with central management, reporting and a design that scales with your EV offering.

For car dealers and garages: charging infrastructure that supports your operations

Demo and test vehicles must always be ready to go. Customers expect to be able to top up their EV during a service or delivery. And employees and service vehicles must not overload the system. Powerland designs your charging infrastructure so that every group gets what it needs — with priority rules, identification per vehicle and clear reporting. Whether it's a showroom with five charging points or a large garage with separate zones: the system scales with your planning.

For leasing companies: one partner from installation to management

Your customers expect a working charging solution, without having to coordinate installer, electrician and management platform themselves. Powerland takes on that role. We carry out the site analysis per customer location, handle the complete installation (electrical and charging infrastructure) and design every site so that expansion is possible without duplicate costs. After handover we remain available: preventive maintenance, remote diagnosis and fast intervention, optionally via SLA. One point of contact, from first conversation to daily operations.

 

Solar panels for dealers and automotive sites

Large roofs, high yield

  • Showroom and workshop roofs are often ideal for PV
  • Own production reduces your cost per kWh
  • Works well combined with daytime charging

PV as part of your sustainability image

  • Clearly visible to your customers
  • Fits with EV positioning and innovation
  • Measurable in kWh and CO₂ impact

PV + charging plaza + EMS: one integrated system

  • Maximum self-consumption through smart control
  • Less grid consumption during opening hours
  • More stable cost picture

Battery storage for peak shaving and stable charging

Buffering peak moments

  • Multiple vehicles charging simultaneously + workshop consumption
  • Less impact from quarter-hour peaks
  • More stable operation of your installation

Limit or phase the impact of grid reinforcement

  • Battery as buffer during peaks (especially with DC)
  • Smarter use of available capacity
  • Phased growth possible

Extra return in combination with PV and EMS

  • Store PV energy during the day for later use
  • EMS controls charging/discharging based on your situation
  • Less grid consumption during expensive periods (depending on contract)
