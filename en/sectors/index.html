Solar panels on barns & sheds

Agricultural businesses often have large roof surfaces and high daytime consumption. This makes solar panels on barns and sheds particularly interesting: you generate electricity at the very moment you typically consume it (cooling, ventilation, pumps, processing).

Benefits

  • Large roof surface → high yield
  • Less dependent on rising energy prices
  • Strong match with cooling and ventilation
  • Greater energy autonomy for your business

Perfectly suited for dairy farms, arable farming, poultry, horticulture and mixed agricultural businesses.

Battery storage can sometimes defer or reduce the need for grid reinforcement, depending on your consumption profile and growth plans.

Battery storage for peak consumption (BESS)

Peak consumption occurs on agricultural businesses when multiple installations run or start up simultaneously. Think of milking robots, cooling tanks, ventilation, irrigation or spray pumps and feeding installations. These peaks increase your costs and can put heavy strain on your grid connection.

Benefits

  • Flatten peak consumption (peak shaving)
  • Avoid costly peak moments
  • Allow installations to run more stably (less voltage drop)
  • Make better use of your existing grid capacity

Smart energy management (EMS) for agricultural businesses

An Energy Management System (EMS) measures and analyses your energy profile and automatically controls consumers based on peaks, available power and (where present) PV production and battery storage. Ideal for agricultural businesses where continuity and operational reliability are essential.

Smart and safe charging on the agricultural business

Charging stations on the farm

A charging station on an agricultural business is typically intended for a private electric car. Powerland installs compact AC charging solutions that take into account your grid connection and your heavy consumers.

With smart load balancing (and EMS where applicable):

  • Charge without overloading or tripped fuses
  • Charge with solar surplus where possible
  • Charge within fixed time windows or with access rules
