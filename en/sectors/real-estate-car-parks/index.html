More value from your roof, your building and your car park

Electric driving and smart energy use are changing the role of buildings and car parks. Residents, tenants and visitors expect charging facilities. At the same time, roofs, car parks and canopies offer enormous opportunities for solar energy and battery storage. Powerland combines solar panels, batteries, charging infrastructure and EMS into one manageable whole, tailored to your type of site.

Charging infrastructure

Charging points for residents, tenants, visitors or public use. Per parking space or shared, with individual billing and scalable as demand grows.

Battery storage

Real estate with car parking

In apartment buildings and office buildings, energy is a shared matter. Power is limited, multiple residents or tenants want to charge and the billing must be correct — per user, per month, transparent for the property manager or owners' association.

Powerland designs installations that work modularly: residents join when they want, without the entire building needing to be equipped at once. Billing is handled automatically via split billing between communal and individual consumption. And the installation is prepared for expansion — adding extra charging points requires no breaking works or new cabling.

  • Charging point per parking space with individual billing
  • Modular entry: one resident can start without everyone joining in
  • Split billing for property manager/owners' association
  • Retrofit in existing buildings without major breaking works
  • EV-ready design for new builds and renovations

Car parks as charging hubs

A car park is no longer just a place to park. Depending on the location, a car park can evolve from "car park with charging stations" to a charging hub where parking is also possible — with its own energy production via solar carports, public charging as a revenue model and scalable infrastructure from 5 to 200+ charging points.

  • Solar carports: canopy with solar panels that power charging points and car park
  • Public charging via QR or payment solution, with idle fees and time limits for throughput
  • AC for long-stay parkers, compact DC for short stops
  • Battery and EMS for peak control during simultaneous charging
  • Scalable design without duplicate costs
