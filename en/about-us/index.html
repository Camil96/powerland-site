About Powerland

Powerland is a Belgian technical energy partner for businesses and private individuals, specialised in charging infrastructure, battery storage, solar panels and energy management. As a division of the Vandotec Group — active in energy logistics since 1978 — Powerland combines nearly five decades of infrastructure experience with the latest technologies in the electric transition.

We carry out the full chain with our own people: from civil works and electrotechnics to installation, management and maintenance. Brand-independent, sustainable and modular — with a single point of contact from advice to handover.

We work like our hummingbird: light, precise and full of energy.

 

1978

Founding of Vandotec. Started as a supplier and specialist in fossil energy infrastructure: petrol stations and industrial fuel systems, with a turnkey focus.

 

2009

Acceleration and expansion under Benoit Goesaert. Vandotec grows further on the basis of energy expertise and expands into CNG/LNG. Complex installations and work to strict standards: that has long been in the DNA. Service and maintenance becomes a key pillar.

 

2020

Launch of Powerland as a division within the Vandotec Group. Building on that solid infrastructure base, a specialised division is established for charging infrastructure and smart energy projects.

 

2023

Powerland becomes total provider & contractor. Powerland grows into integrated projects combining charging infrastructure, BESS and solar. From study and engineering to installation and handover: one point of contact, one plan, one execution.

 

Today

From installation to energy that delivers returns. Powerland realises integrated projects in which charging infrastructure, BESS and solar are designed and controlled together, with a focus on reliability, peak reduction and return on investment.
